# CreditRoadmap Monetisation Opportunities

Date: 2026-08-28

No affiliate links were added in this expansion.

## Future ethical opportunities

- Credit report access and monitoring: strongest fit on credit-report and correction pages, with careful disclosure and no score-improvement promises.
- Eligibility/pre-application tools: possible fit on credit-card readiness, car-finance readiness and loan-readiness pages, provided links do not imply guaranteed approval.
- Mortgage readiness services: possible fit on mortgage-readiness pages only with clear distinction between education and regulated advice.
- Car finance eligibility: commercially relevant but needs cautious compliance language and no lender-affiliation claims.
- Tenant referencing support: possible future category, but lower priority until search signals appear.

## Avoid for now

- Payday/high-cost credit promotion.
- Debt solution lead generation without specialist compliance review.
- Claims that any provider will accept users with CCJs, defaults or bankruptcy.
